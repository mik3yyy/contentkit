## AD SCRIPT 1 — "UGC Pointing at Screen" Style

**Platform:** TikTok / Reels / Shorts
**Length:** 45–60 sec
**Tone:** Casual, authentic, FOMO-driven

### Visual Direction
Creator sits/stands in front of their laptop screen showing the ContentKit website. They alternate between talking to camera and pointing at the screen. Fast cuts to the actual niche video previews playing on screen.

---

**[0:00–0:03] — HOOK**

> *[Creator looks shocked, points directly at camera]*

**VO:** *"Wait — you can legally resell ALL of this and keep 100% of the money?"*

**TEXT OVERLAY:** `"This is actually legal 👀"`

---

**[0:03–0:10] — PROBLEM**

> *[Cut to creator looking tired/frustrated]*

**VO:** *"If you're still spending hours making content from scratch or paying freelancers every month… stop. Because I found the cheat code."*

**TEXT OVERLAY:** `"Stop wasting time & money on content"`

---

**[0:10–0:25] — THE REVEAL (point at screen)**

> *[Creator turns, points at ContentKit website — camera shows niches loading: Fitness, Gaming, Finance, Lifestyle]*

**VO:** *"This is ContentKit. One payment. Lifetime access to over 100,000 HD and 4K videos — sorted by niche. Fitness. Finance. Gaming. Lifestyle. Motivation. And 500+ ebooks you can literally rebrand and resell as your own."*

> *[Click on a video — it plays a preview clip in full screen]*

**TEXT OVERLAY:** `"100,000+ videos | 500+ ebooks | 50+ niches"`

---

**[0:25–0:38] — THE MONEY CLAIM**

> *[Creator leans in close to camera, excited]*

**VO:** *"Here's the crazy part. These come with full commercial AND resell rights. People are flipping these on Etsy, Gumroad, Stan Store — making a thousand dollars a month or more. And with ONE click…"*

> *[Shows download button being clicked — zip file downloading]*

**VO:** *"…it downloads as a clean organised zip. Ready to use, post, or sell. Immediately."*

**TEXT OVERLAY:** `"$1,000+/month just reselling ✅"`

---

**[0:38–0:50] — FREE SAMPLE CTA**

> *[Creator holds up hand like "wait, one more thing"]*

**VO:** *"And before you even think about paying — they're giving away 100 free videos right now. Different niches, different folders, just to let you try it. No credit card. Just drop your email and it downloads."*

> *[Screen shows the Free Sample section on the landing page — email being typed, download button clicked]*

**TEXT OVERLAY:** `"100 FREE videos → no card needed"`

---

**[0:50–0:60] — CLOSE**

> *[Creator back to camera, direct eye contact]*

**VO:** *"One payment. Everything unlocked. Forever. Link in bio."*

**TEXT OVERLAY:** `"Pay once. Own it forever. 🔓"`

## AD SCRIPT 2 — "Pattern Interrupt / Fast Cut" Style

**Platform:** TikTok / Reels / YouTube Pre-roll
**Length:** 30–45 sec
**Tone:** Bold, fast, high-energy

### Visual Direction
Opens on pure black screen. Rapid fire cuts between preview clips from each niche (2–3 seconds each). No talking head — pure screen + voiceover + aggressive text overlays. Cinematic feel.

---

**[0:00–0:03] — HOOK**

> *[Black screen. White text slams in]*

**TEXT:** `"WHAT IF YOU NEVER HAD TO MAKE CONTENT AGAIN."`

**VO (deep, calm):** *"What if someone just… handed you the library."*

---

**[0:03–0:12] — FAST NICHE MONTAGE**

> *[Rapid cuts: Fitness clip → Finance clip → Gaming clip → Lifestyle clip → Motivation clip → Ebook covers flying in]*

**VO:** *"100,000 HD and 4K videos. 500 plus ebooks. Fitness, finance, gaming, lifestyle, motivation — every niche you could need. All in one place."*

**TEXT OVERLAYS (each cut):**
- `"FITNESS 💪"`
- `"MONEY & FINANCE 💰"`
- `"GAMING 🎮"`
- `"LIFESTYLE ✨"`
- `"MOTIVATION 🔥"`
- `"500+ EBOOKS 📚"`

---

**[0:12–0:22] — THE RESELL ANGLE**

> *[Slow zoom into ContentKit dashboard — download button visible]*

**VO:** *"Full commercial licence. Full resell rights. One click downloads as an organised zip — ready to post, bundle, or sell. People are making a thousand dollars a month just reselling these on their own stores."*

**TEXT:** `"POST IT. SELL IT. KEEP 100% 💵"`

---

**[0:22–0:32] — OFFER + FREE SAMPLE**

> *[Cut to pricing section — $12 price animates in]*

**VO:** *"Twelve dollars. One time. Lifetime access. And if you want proof before you pay — grab the 100 free sample videos right now."*

**TEXT:** `"$12 ONE TIME 🔓"`

**TEXT:** `"OR grab 100 FREE videos first ↓"`

---

**[0:32–0:38] — FINAL CTA**

> *[Black screen — single white line text]*

**TEXT:** `"ContentKit. Pay once. Use forever."`

**TEXT:** `"Link in bio → free samples waiting"`

**VO:** *"The library is ready. Are you?"*

## Hooks to A/B Test

| # | Hook | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | *"Wait — this is actually legal?"* | Curiosity + disbelief pattern interrupt |
| 2 | *"POV: You stopped making content from scratch"* | Aspirational POV format |
| 3 | *"I paid $12 and now I never run out of content"* | Specific number = credibility |
| 4 | *"This website is why some creators never film anything"* | Intrigue + insider knowledge |
| 5 | *"The plug nobody in your niche wants you to find"* | FOMO + exclusivity |
| 6 | *"You're allowed to download this, resell it, and keep all the money"* | Disbelief + income claim |

---

## Recommendation

Run **Script 1** first — the pointing-at-screen UGC format is the #1 converting format for digital products right now because it feels real and shows the product in action. Use Hook #3 or #6 to open it. Then test Script 2 as the polished version once you have proof of concept.
